About Without Permission.

Independent Egyptian feminist magazine. Originally RAW by Vee.

We are not here to be comfortable.

Without Permission makes space for writing, analysis and questioning inherited power structures.

Your voice does not need permission.

Editorial principles

Use original sources, distinguish evidence from interpretation, respect privacy and consent, and make corrections transparent.

From RAW by Vee

Previously published work stays in Vee Archive with its original titles, texts and links.
